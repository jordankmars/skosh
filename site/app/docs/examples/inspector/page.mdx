# Inspector

<Lead>Panels of fields that drive something on screen.</Lead>

Drag any label, or click into a value and type.

## Transform panel

Position, size, rotation and radius on one shape. Leave out `min` and `max` for values that can go anywhere, and set them for the ones that can't.

<Example name="transform-panel" />

## Typography

Small decimals need a small `perPx`. `precision` keeps line height and letter spacing to two places.

<Example name="typography" />

## Audio mixer

A wide range and a narrow one side by side. Frequency moves 10 Hz per pixel, gain runs below zero, and Q moves slowly.

<Example name="audio-mixer" />

## Undo history

`onValueChange` fires for every step of a drag, and `onValueCommit` fires once at the end. Add to the history on commit, and a whole drag is one undo.

<Example name="undo-history" />
